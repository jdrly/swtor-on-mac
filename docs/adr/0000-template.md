# Decision title written as a statement

## Context

What forced this decision: the problem, the evidence (measurements, crashes, logs, a player's
report) and why it can't wait. Link the issues, PRs and documents it comes from.

## Decision

State what applies, in the present tense. Avoid “should” and “we will consider”.

## Consequences

The effects on players, performance, correctness, the build, releases and maintenance,
**including the unpleasant ones**. An ADR without a disadvantage usually means the alternatives
were not weighed honestly.

## Alternatives considered

At least two rejected alternatives, and why they were not chosen.

## Open questions

Optional. What is not decided yet or not verified, so nobody reads it as settled.

## Implementation status as of YYYY-MM-DD

Optional; add later. Link the code and tests that implement the decision and list known gaps.
