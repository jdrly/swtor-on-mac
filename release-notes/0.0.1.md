## SWTOR on Mac 0.0.1

First test version.

- Installs SWTOR on Mac on top of the official SWTOR Mac app: d9mt (Direct3D 9 on Metal) on
  CrossOver 24.0.7 Wine, macOS Game Mode, Retina mode, the mouse-look fix.
- Settings (⌘,): Retina mode, vsync, video memory profile, Game Mode, Metal HUD, debug logging.
- Updates itself (Check for Updates…), and the game's start looks for a new version once a day.
- Hooks itself back in when the official launcher updates its Wine.
- Collect Logs… for bug reports, without account names or passwords.
- Uninstall restores the official app exactly as Broadsword ships it and copies your game
  settings back to it.

The first minutes of play (and each new area or effect seen for the first time) may stutter
while shaders are compiled and cached; it gets smooth after that. After an update of SWTOR on
Mac the cache is rebuilt once.
