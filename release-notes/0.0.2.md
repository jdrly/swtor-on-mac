## SWTOR on Mac 0.0.2

Clearer wording of the notice; no functional changes.

- The notice (before you install, and behind the *Unofficial community project* badge) now
  says plainly what SWTOR on Mac is: not a game mod. It doesn't change the game's files,
  memory or network traffic; it only replaces the compatibility layer the official Mac app
  already runs on (Wine, plus d9mt for Direct3D 9 on Metal).

### Support
SWTOR on Mac is an unofficial project, not made, endorsed or supported by Broadsword Games, EA,
Lucasfilm or Disney, and you use it at your own risk. Please send questions and bug reports to
Pharaphobia on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AnV3fZWz2b) or as a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jdrly/swtor-on-mac/issues),
never to Broadsword or SWTOR customer support.
