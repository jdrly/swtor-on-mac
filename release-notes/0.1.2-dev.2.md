## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.2-dev.2 (development build)

The second test build of 0.1.2: the fixes of 0.1.2-dev.1, the game window coming to the front by itself, and the final version of the Cartel Market fix.

### The Cartel Market is no longer pink, and the game no longer freezes
With 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 the Cartel Market and other overlay windows could flicker or turn pink,
and the game could freeze. The graphics driver freed some textures while the GPU still needed
them. d9mt (`c3f46ce`) now keeps them until the GPU is done with them. This build has the
final, reviewed version of that fix; in our measurements it is nowhere slower than 0.1.1.

### The game window comes to the front by itself
When the game starts, its window now comes to the front by itself: SWTOR on Mac lets the game
take over instead of staying in front.

### The launcher closes by itself again
The SWTOR launcher behaves like the original again. It closes by itself after the game starts,
no more frozen launcher in the background, and no "Running in Background" icon stuck in your
Dock after you quit.
- No more leftover Wine processes after playing, and the app stops telling you Wine is still
  running when it isn't.

### A stability fix deep inside the Wine engine
In the CrossOver 26 engine, a thread that ended could close a file that wasn't its own: a game
file, a connection to Wine's server, or something Wine waits on, which can make loads fail or
programs hang. SWTOR on Mac now ships Wine's `ntdll.so` with that fixed
(`patches/ntdll-msync-alert-fd.patch`, the same fix athei made on another branch). Everything
else in the engine stays the same.

### Known issues
- The launcher plays a short beep when it closes. That's Broadsword's launcher doing it.
- If you reopen the Cartel Market right after closing it, the game sometimes ignores that press.
  The game does this on Windows too.

### Please test
Open the Cartel Market a few times (Ctrl+M and the menu), look at other overlay windows, play
as usual for a while, then quit: does the launcher close by itself after the game starts, and
is the Dock clear after you quit? If something goes wrong, use *Collect Logs…* (it includes the crash reports now)
and send it.

### After updating
Quit SWTOR and the launcher, copy the app from the DMG over the one in Applications, open it and
click *Update to 0.1.2-dev.2*. From 0.1.2-dev.1, 0.1.1 or 0.1.0 this takes under a minute and
downloads nothing. From 0.0.6 it downloads the new Wine engine once (about 235 MB) and updates your Wine
setup for it; your game settings, key bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay. This is a
development build: it isn't offered as an update, Debug Logging is on by default, and there
are no automatic update checks.
