## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.2

The new Wine engine, smoother mouse-look and less stutter of 0.1, now without the pink Cartel Market, with a launcher that behaves like the original, and with a stability fix inside the Wine engine.

### The Cartel Market is no longer pink, and the game no longer freezes
With 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 the Cartel Market and other overlay windows could flicker or turn pink,
and the game could freeze. The graphics driver freed some textures while the GPU still needed
them. d9mt (`2a1bc00`) now keeps them until the GPU is done with them. In our measurements it is
nowhere slower than 0.1.1.

### The launcher closes by itself again
The SWTOR launcher behaves like the original again. It closes by itself after the game starts,
no more frozen launcher in the background, and no "Running in Background" icon stuck in your
Dock after you quit.
- No more leftover Wine processes after playing, and the app stops telling you Wine is still
  running when it isn't.
- The game window comes to the front by itself when the game starts.

### A stability fix deep inside the Wine engine
In the CrossOver 26 engine, a thread that ended could close a file that wasn't its own: a game
file, a connection to Wine's server, or something Wine waits on, which can make loads fail or
programs hang. SWTOR on Mac now ships Wine's `ntdll.so` with that fixed
(`patches/ntdll-msync-alert-fd.patch`, the same fix athei made on another branch).

### New since 0.0.6: a new Wine engine
SWTOR on Mac now runs the game on CrossOver 26.3 with Wine 11, the same engine SWGL on Mac
uses, instead of CrossOver 24.0.7 with Wine 9. Game Mode, Retina, vsync and the video memory
profiles work as before, and so does Settings > Keyboard > *Game keys by keyboard position*.

### New since 0.0.6: smoother mouse-look
While you hold a mouse button to turn the camera, the game gets every movement of the mouse as
it happens, straight from the mouse. The pointer stays where it is while you turn and comes
back where you clicked when you let go.
- It is on by default. Settings > Mouse > *Raw mouse-look* turns it off.
- *Mouse-Look Speed* (0.5× to 3×) sets how fast the camera turns. At 1× it turns as fast as
  your pointer moves.

### New since 0.0.6: less stutter and less GPU load
- The graphics driver remembers which pipelines (the GPU programs for each way the game draws)
  the game uses, and prepares them in the background while you log in. Your first session
  makes the list; from then on, busy places stutter less, also after future updates.
- At high resolutions the GPU does less work for the same frames: in testing at 4K the game
  kept its frame rate with about a third less GPU power. That matters most on base M-series
  chips and fanless MacBook Airs.

### Easier bug reports
*Collect Logs…* now also adds macOS's crash reports of the game, Wine and SWTOR on Mac from the
last 7 days to the zip, so you don't have to look for them.

### Good to know
- The launcher plays a short beep when it closes. That's Broadsword's launcher doing it.
- If you reopen the Cartel Market right after closing it, the game sometimes ignores that press.
  The game does this on Windows too.
- If you use Apple's Metal HUD (Settings > Game > *Show Metal HUD*), it now sits at the top
  center by default; Settings > Game > *Metal HUD Position* moves it. In the game Shift+F9 hides
  and shows it. Shift+F12, or three quick clicks on the HUD, open macOS's own HUD settings
  panel, even while the HUD is hidden.

### After updating
Open SWTOR on Mac and click *Update to 0.1.2* in the window, with the launcher and the game
closed.
- From 0.0.6 it downloads the new Wine engine once (about 235 MB) and updates your Wine setup
  for it in under a minute. If the update fails, everything is put back as it was.
- From 0.1.0 or 0.1.1 it takes under a minute and downloads nothing.

Your game settings, key bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay, and so does your
compiled-shader cache, so there is no extra stutter after the update.
