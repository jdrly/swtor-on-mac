## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.3

A fix for the update from 0.0.6: the one-time download of the new Wine engine no longer fails with "damaged (checksum mismatch)" when something on the way breaks it.

### The engine download is sturdier
When you update from 0.0.6, SWTOR on Mac downloads the new Wine engine once (about 235 MB).
For some players that download ended with "The download of Wine engine is damaged (checksum
mismatch)", every time they tried. SWTOR on Mac now:
- picks up a download that breaks off where it stopped, instead of starting over or giving up;
- fetches the file a second way through GitHub if the first one brings a damaged copy;
- tells you what went wrong: a web page instead of the file (hotel or public Wi-Fi, a proxy, a
  VPN or a content filter), a download that stopped, or a file changed on the way, with how
  much arrived.

If it still can't get the file, you can download it yourself: the message has the link. Put the
file into `~/Library/Caches/SWTOR on Mac/downloads` and click *Update to 0.1.3* again.

Nothing else changes: the graphics driver, the Wine engine and your settings are the same as in
0.1.2.

### After updating
From 0.0.6: open SWTOR on Mac and click *Update to 0.1.3* in the window, with the launcher and
the game closed. It downloads the new Wine engine once and updates your Wine setup for it in
under a minute; your game settings, key bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay. From 0.1.2:
click *Update to 0.1.3*; it takes under a minute and downloads nothing.
