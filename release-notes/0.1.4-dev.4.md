## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.4-dev.4 (development build)

A test build of everything planned for 0.1.4: a new look, no background helper, a frame rate limit, GPU errors in *Collect Logs…* and a newer graphics driver.

### A new look
The window takes on the look of [swonmac.com](https://swonmac.com): its colours, type and a
background picture behind the window's content, which stays still in 0.1.4. Settings keep their
usual macOS look, and their sidebar shows the version at its foot.

### No more background helper
SWTOR on Mac no longer runs anything while it's closed. Until now, a small helper (a launchd
job) put SWTOR on Mac back into the launcher whenever the launcher updated its Wine. Now the app
does that itself, each time you open it. After a launcher update, open SWTOR on Mac once (or press
*Play SWTOR* in it). If you start SWTOR from the official app right after such an update, the game
runs without SWTOR on Mac until you have opened SWTOR on Mac once. The update (*Update to
0.1.4-dev.4*, below) removes the old helper.

### A frame rate limit
Settings > Graphics > *Frame Rate Limit* (Off, 30, 40, 45, 60) caps the game's frame rate for
less heat and a longer battery life, with even frame times. *Only on battery* applies it only
when the Mac runs on its battery as the game starts.

### GPU errors in Collect Logs
*Collect Logs…* also adds `gpu-errors.txt`, with the GPU errors of the game and Wine from macOS's
log of the last 2 hours. `system-info.txt` shows how large macOS's Metal caches for the game have
grown. It takes 10 to 30 seconds; meanwhile the menu item says *Collecting Logs…*.

### The launcher's update
Launcher 7.2.207 renamed itself inside (its bundle id). SWTOR on Mac recognises it under both
names, so *Play SWTOR* and *Show SWTOR Launcher* keep working.

### The graphics driver
d9mt `b0d4597`:
- No more pink frames or GPU errors right after the game starts.
- Pipelines are prepared with less work.
- It can report dropped GPU work with a `D9MT-GPU-LOST` line in `wine.log`. It checks a sample
  of the GPU's work, so not every fault gets a line.
- The frame cap that the Frame Rate Limit uses.
- The list of pipelines it prepares in the background starts over once after the update.

### After updating
Quit the SWTOR launcher and the game, open SWTOR on Mac and click *Update to 0.1.4-dev.4* in the
window. That puts the new graphics driver in place and removes the old helper. Your game
settings, key bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay.
