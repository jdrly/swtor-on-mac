## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.4

A new look, no background helper, a frame rate limit, GPU errors in *Collect Logs…*, and a newer graphics driver: no more pink frames or GPU errors right after the game starts.

### A new look
The window takes on the look of [swonmac.com](https://swonmac.com): its colours, type and a
background picture behind the window's content, which stays still in 0.1.4. Settings keep their
usual macOS look, and their sidebar shows the version at its foot.

### No more background helper
SWTOR on Mac no longer runs anything while it's closed. Until now, a small helper (a launchd
job) put SWTOR on Mac back into the launcher whenever the launcher updated its Wine. Now the app
does that itself, each time you open it. After a launcher update, open SWTOR on Mac once (or press
*Play SWTOR* in it). If you start SWTOR from the official app right after such an update, the game
runs without SWTOR on Mac until you have opened SWTOR on Mac once. The update (*Update to
0.1.4*, below) removes the old helper.

### A frame rate limit
Settings > Graphics > *Frame Rate Limit* (Off, 30, 40, 45, 60) caps the game's frame rate for
less heat and a longer battery life, with even frame times. *Only on battery* applies it only
when the Mac runs on its battery as the game starts.

### GPU errors in Collect Logs
*Collect Logs…* also adds `gpu-errors.txt`, with the GPU errors of the game and Wine from macOS's
log of the last 2 hours, because `wine.log` starts anew with every game start. `system-info.txt`
shows how large macOS's Metal caches for the game have grown. It takes 10 to 30 seconds;
meanwhile the menu item says *Collecting Logs…*.

### The launcher's update
Launcher 7.2.207 renamed itself inside (its bundle id). SWTOR on Mac recognises it under both
names, so *Play SWTOR* and *Show SWTOR Launcher* keep working.

### No more pink frames or GPU errors when the game starts
Right after the game started, the graphics driver could let go of the screen's image before the
GPU had finished with it. That could show pink frames or end in a GPU error. d9mt (`b0d4597`) now
keeps the image until the GPU is done.

### The graphics driver does less work for the same frames
- Each pipeline (the GPU program for one way the game draws) is now built and recorded once,
  instead of up to three times, and found again faster.
- Changing what kind of shapes the game draws no longer forces a pipeline lookup.
- The list of pipelines the driver prepares in the background now survives a crash or a force
  quit of the game.
- Fixes in rarely used drawing paths (old-style effects without a texture, unused resource
  slots).
- While the game is in the background, the driver slows it down with less CPU work of its own.

### A clearer log when the GPU drops work
When the GPU throws away a piece of work (a GPU fault), `wine.log` can now say so with a
`D9MT-GPU-LOST` line. The driver checks a sample of the GPU's work, so not every fault gets a
line; macOS's own log still has them. That helps when you report graphics problems with
*Collect Logs…*.

### One time: the pipeline list is made again
The driver now records pipelines in a new way, so the list it prepares in the background starts
over once. Your first session after the update may stutter a little more in busy places while it
records them again; from the second session on, they are prepared while you log in, as before.
Your compiled-shader cache is kept.

### Debug: the THREADCPU wineserver figure was 41.7× too small
The Mac driver's THREADCPU debug line (`warn+threadcpu`, for performance reports) showed the
wineserver's CPU time 41.7 times too small on Apple silicon: macOS reports a process's CPU time
in clock ticks of 41.67 ns, which the driver read as nanoseconds. It now converts them. Nothing
changes for playing; only performance reports that compare that figure are affected
(`patches/winemac-swtor-mouse.patch`).

### After updating
Open SWTOR on Mac and click *Update to 0.1.4* in the window, with the launcher and the game
closed. It takes under a minute and downloads nothing (from 0.0.6 it downloads the new Wine engine
once, about 235 MB). It puts the new graphics driver in place and removes the old helper. Your
game settings, key bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay.
