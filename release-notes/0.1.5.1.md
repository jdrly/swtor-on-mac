## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.5.1

A fix for the game that hangs without a window after you press Play: SWTOR on Mac now tells you when macOS caused it and which setting fixes it.

### When the launcher closes and the game never shows up
About 30 seconds after you press Play, the SWTOR launcher quits. If macOS doesn't allow the
launcher to run in the background, macOS then ends everything the launcher left running,
including the Wine that the game needs, and the game hangs without a window. SWTOR on Mac now
spots this in macOS's own log and tells you:
- when SWTOR on Mac opens, a notice in its window if it happened in the last 24 hours, with a
  button that opens *Login Items & Extensions* in System Settings. *Dismiss* hides it until it
  happens again;
- the next time you press Play after it happened, a macOS notification with the same advice;
- *Collect Logs…* says whether it happened in the last 7 days.

To fix it, open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions, and under *Allow in the
Background* turn on Star Wars - The Old Republic (it may be listed as Broadsword). Then press Play
again.

Nothing else changes: the Wine engine, the graphics driver and your settings are the same as in
0.1.5. If you're updating from 0.1.4 or older, 0.1.5.1 also brings everything in
[0.1.5](https://github.com/jdrly/swtor-on-mac/releases/tag/v0.1.5).

### After updating
Open SWTOR on Mac and click *Update to 0.1.5.1* in the window, with the launcher and the game
closed. From 0.1.5 it takes under a minute and downloads nothing; it puts the new notification in
place. Your game settings, key bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay.
