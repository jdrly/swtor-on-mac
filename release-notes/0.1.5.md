## SWTOR on Mac 0.1.5

A fix for 0.1.4's GPU error check: its cost on frames that aren't shown is gone, as far as we can measure, and it checks every frame you see.

### Less overhead from the GPU error check
0.1.4 added a check that lets `wine.log` say so with a `D9MT-GPU-LOST` line when the GPU throws
away a piece of work. It gave every fourth piece of the game's GPU work a small extra task of its
own, also when that work put no frame on the screen. In a test that draws without showing its
frames, that cost about 1.5 % of the frame rate; in the same test, 0.1.5 is as fast as 0.1.3 was
before the check. Now the graphics driver (d9mt `f6cfa71`):
- checks every frame shown on the screen. A frame copied to the screen carries the check in the
  copy task it already has; one that is scaled to the window or upscaled with MetalFX gets a tiny
  task of its own. In 0.1.4, those scaled frames were only checked now and then.
- adds a task of its own to work that shows no frame only when nothing was checked for a quarter
  of a second, so at most four times a second.

Nothing else changes: the Wine engine, the rest of the graphics driver and your settings are the
same as in 0.1.4. If you're updating from 0.1.3 or older, 0.1.5 also brings everything in
[0.1.4](https://github.com/jdrly/swtor-on-mac/releases/tag/v0.1.4): a new look, no background
helper, a frame rate limit and a newer graphics driver.

### Known issue: a rare graphics error can freeze the whole screen
Rarely, a graphics error in the game freezes the whole screen, not only the game. macOS may
restart the graphical session after a short delay and return you to the login screen. Open apps
may close and unsaved work may be lost; the game can remain running. A fix is being worked on
for a later version. If you see it, open SWTOR on Mac again soon afterwards, choose
*Collect Logs…* and send the zip: it holds the GPU errors of the last 2 hours.

### After updating
Open SWTOR on Mac and click *Update to 0.1.5* in the window, with the launcher and the game
closed. From 0.1.4 it normally takes under a minute and downloads nothing; it puts the new
graphics driver in place. From 0.1.3 or older it also removes the old helper, and from 0.0.6 it
downloads the new Wine engine once, about 235 MB, which may take longer. Your game settings, key
bindings and SWTOR on Mac's settings stay.
